## What was built

- **Quest catalog + daily selection (§6.7 / D12):** all **12 quest definitions** seed into the `quests` collection with `id`, `title`, `description`, `type`, `target`, `xpReward` (20–50), `coinReward` (5–15), `phase`, and `order`. Three unique quests are chosen per user + local day by a deterministic seeded shuffle of `userId:date`; rows persist in `userQuests` and reset only when the local date key changes. Phase 5 selects the **8 review-completable** defs; the 4 quiz defs are seeded as `phase: 6` and become selectable when quiz events exist.
- **Progress tracking + rewards (§6.7 / §11.1):** `trackQuestEvent` applies each review to the day's rows — review counts, 5-in-a-row reset/completion, first-time learns, daily XP threshold, due-queue clear, and before-10am/after-8pm local-hour quests. Completion is a single-claim transition (`rewardClaimed` guard), pays the quest's XP/coins, fires the **daily sweep +30 XP** once when all three are done, and evaluates achievements in the same pass (`Gōkaku 合格` now activates). Quest/sweep XP counts toward `totalXP` and level (1.5× boost at award) but **not** the daily goal or streak.
- **Undo integration (§6.5):** each review history entry stores a `questUndo` snapshot of the three rows before the event plus the direct quest/sweep XP and coins paid. Undo restores progress/completion/reward-claim/sweep flags and refunds those rewards exactly; achievement unlocks remain permanent.
- **Statistics service + endpoints (§7.7 / §9):** local-day buckets for the **365-day heatmap**, **30-day forecast** (the _next_ 30 days, overdue due cards folded into today), **30-day accuracy** (Good/Easy = rating ≥ 2), **JLPT N5–N3 stacked statuses** over all seeded kanji (untracked counted as `new`), and **best/worst days** over 365 days with a ≥10-review floor. `/stats` returns all five sections; `/stats/heatmap`, `/forecast`, `/accuracy`, `/jlpt`, and `/best-worst` are independently queryable with Zod-validated ranges (422 on bad input).
- **Dashboard quest card (§7.1):** one section under the due card with the exact “Daily quests” heading, local-midnight countdown (“Resets in 6h 42m”), three stacked cards (custom icon, title, description, 4px progress bar, progress count, XP + coins), completed styling (subtle background, strikethrough, matcha check + awarded XP), and the sweep line (“Complete all three for +30 XP.” → “Daily sweep complete — +30 XP”). Loading skeletons and an error/retry state included; the stale duplicate quest section was removed.
- **`/stats` screen (§7.7):** inside `AppShell`, the five spec sections — full-width 365-day heatmap with month/weekday labels and hover counts, accuracy line chart, forecast bar chart, N5–N3 stacked JLPT bars with legend, and best/worst day lists — plus the 30-day accuracy average, skeletons, and an error state. No percentages or colors outside the design tokens.
- **Review feedback:** quest completion and sweep toasts on `/review`, with quests + stats query invalidation so the dashboard and stats pages refresh after a session.
- **Data model:** new `QuestDef` (`quests`) and `UserQuest` (`userQuests`) collections with a unique `{ userId, date, questId }` index and `{ userId, date }` lookup index; `UserProgress.history[]` gains the optional `questUndo` snapshot. `scripts/seed-quests.js` seeds idempotently and `scripts/migrate-060.js` ensures indexes.
